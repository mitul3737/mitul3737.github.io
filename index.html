Guide yourself

Tech is far bigger than web development, app development, video editing and competitive programming. Cloud, data, security, research, embedded systems and more are open to you.

Two halves. Same starting point.

Most CS students in Bangladesh end up choosing between building a career in industry and going abroad for a master's or PhD. Pick a path to see its stops. Nobody is waiting at the end to grade you.

Before you pick

Spend one day researching, then decide

Whether you're in first year or have already graduated and are moving into software engineering, start the same way.

Read the trends

Look at where industry and research are heading this year, globally and in Bangladesh.

Think it through with AI

Use an AI assistant to question your assumptions and fill in what you don't know yet.

Talk to someone in industry

One honest conversation with a working engineer tells you more than ten forum threads.

Talk to a professor abroad

Ask what their students did before joining, and what they do after.

Then make the call yourself. Nobody knows where the world is going, or which path suits you, better than you do. Other people can give you information. The decision is yours.

    This is a map, not a syllabus. Years are rough. Skip stops you've passed, stay longer where you need to, and switch paths whenever you want. Ask seniors on Discord when you're stuck on a specific problem.

    Think in orders of magnitude5,000years

    One idea can be worth more than a lifetime of salary. Several lifetimes, in fact.

    At ৳20 lakh a month, the top end of corporate pay in Bangladesh, it would take about 5,000 years to earn one billion dollars. A product that solves a real problem for enough people can create that much value in a decade.

    ৳20 lakh × 12 = ৳2.4 crore a year · $1 billion ≈ ৳12,000 crore at ~৳120/$ · 12,000 ÷ 2.4 = 5,000 years

    Real example · Cursor

    Cursor was started by four MIT students in 2022. One of them, Sualeh Asif, is from Karachi. By November 2025, Forbes estimated each co-founder's stake at over $1.3 billion, at age 25 or 26. In August 2026, SpaceX completed its $60 billion acquisition of the company, which Forbes estimated would put each founder at about $2.7 billion.

    Forbes: Cursor's co-founders are now billionaires SpaceX deal doubles their net worth The story on LinkedIn

    Most ventures fail, and that is part of the deal. You only find out by building. Whichever path you choose, keep a side project going.

      Programs

      Community programs you can grow into

      Ambassador, champion and builder programs run by the companies behind the tools you use. They bring credits, swag, early access and a direct line to the product team. Most look for a track record: talks, blog posts, events or open-source work.

      Applications open and close in cohorts. If a page says closed, join the waitlist and keep building in public until it reopens.

      Culture

      What we're changing in our own community

      Recognition

      Appreciate what should be appreciated

      When a student ships a project, fixes a bug in someone else's repo, or gives their first talk, we say so out loud. Credit goes to the people who did the work, not the loudest name in the room.

      Visibility

      Use X and LinkedIn well

      Post what you built and what you learned, not just certificates. Follow builders, share their work, and treat your profile as a public notebook that recruiters and professors will actually read.

      Free events

      No catch. No edtech.

      Our events are free and unpaid. There's no course to buy at the end, no "premium batch", and no registration fee hidden behind a form. Sponsors support the room, not a sales pitch.
